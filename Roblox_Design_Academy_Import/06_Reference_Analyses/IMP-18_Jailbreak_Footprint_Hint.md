## Context
A prisoner (orange jumpsuit) stands in a cell area. Black footprint decals lead across the floor to a vent or hatch cover.

## What Works
- **It implies rather than instructs.** Nobody says "escape through the vent", but the footprints tell the story of someone who did. Roblox: hints let players "solve the problem themselves, and feel clever for figuring it out."
- It's diegetic, cheap (one decal) and fits the theme (prison escape).

## Reusable Design Rules
1. For discovery-type goals, use **environmental clues** (footprints, scratch marks, light from a gap, a dropped item) instead of arrows.
2. Use explicit arrows only after a **timed delay** for players who are stuck (see [IMP-21](IMP-21_Plant_Timed_Hints.md)).

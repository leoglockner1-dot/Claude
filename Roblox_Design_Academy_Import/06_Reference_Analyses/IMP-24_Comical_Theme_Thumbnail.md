# Reference IMP-24 — Comical Theme Thumbnail (Roblox Example)

## Metadata
- Category: Thumbnails / Tone
- Source: Roblox Creator Docs, "Thumbnails" > Theme and color ("Comical theme/character with bright colors")
- Asset: `../Assets/Thumbnails/IMP-24_comical-theme.jpg`
- Date added: 2026-10-24
- Rating: 2.5/5
- Confidence: Medium. Whether it is AI-generated is **my visual judgement, not verified**.
- Reference type: Mixed. It works for tone and fails for clarity.

## Context
A cartoon man with huge eyes balances a tower of about 12 hats on his head, hands on hips. Teapots, kettles, vases, shoes and more hats are scattered on the ground around him. Behind is a lake, pine forest, mountains and a village.

## What Works
- **Tone is unmistakable**: bright saturated colours, exaggerated expression and an absurd premise all say "silly, light-hearted". That's what Roblox uses it to illustrate.
- A central subject in a symmetrical, stable composition.

## What Does Not Work
- **No mechanic is communicated.** Is it stacking? Collecting? A shop? The viewer can't tell what they'd *do*.
- **Prop clutter.** About 20 unrelated objects spread around the edges compete with the subject and blur the meaning.
- **It reads as generic AI illustration** (my judgement): painterly default lighting, random object scatter, uncanny face, a picture-book background with no relation to the subject, and no Roblox avatar or gameplay. This is the look the academy wants to avoid.

## Reusable Design Rules
1. The thumbnail must show **verb + subject**, not just a mood: what is the player doing?
2. **Every prop must support the idea.** Remove anything you can't justify.
3. Signs of AI-looking art to check for:
   - props scattered symmetrically for no reason
   - a background unrelated to the subject
   - over-smooth painterly rendering
   - an uncanny face
   - no game-specific detail

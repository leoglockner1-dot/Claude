## Context
A low-angle view up the stairs to a craftsman-style house at night. Dark foliage frames the image. Above the roof, a purple vortex with a glowing eye shoots lightning down.

## What Works
- **One focal point**: the bright purple eye and vortex, the only saturated, brightest area, placed top-centre.
- **Framing**: the dark foreground foliage acts as a natural vignette and pushes the eye inward.
- **One accent hue** (purple) against a desaturated dark scene, with tiny warm porch lamps as secondary contrast.
- **A story question**: what is the eye? Why is it over this house? It provokes curiosity, not just a mood.
- **It's an in-engine render**, so it honestly represents the visuals.

## What Does Not Work
- No character, so the player's role (investigate? survive?) is implied, not shown.
- Much of the image is near-black, and detail in the shadows will disappear at small sizes. The silhouette of the house and the eye survive.

## Reusable Design Rules
1. **One accent colour, everything else muted.** The accent goes where the eye should land.
2. **Frame with dark foreground shapes** to create depth and focus.
3. **Pose a question** (a threat, a mystery, a reward) instead of describing everything.
4. A low camera angle makes the location look imposing.

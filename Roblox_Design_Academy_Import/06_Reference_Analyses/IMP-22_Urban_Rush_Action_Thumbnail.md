## Context
A sports car silhouette (black with white rim light, glowing wheels and headlight) races along a highway made of red and white long-exposure light trails. A helicopter silhouette with a red glow chases it from the upper right.

## What Works
- **The genre is readable in a fraction of a second**: speed (light streaks and diagonals) plus chase (helicopter pursuing).
- **Two subjects, clearly ranked.** The car is large and lower-left. The helicopter is smaller and upper-right. The diagonal composition leads the eye from one to the other.
- **Silhouette and rim light** separate the subjects from a busy background, and they still read at small size.
- A limited palette (black, red, white) gives it a strong mood.

## What Does Not Work / Caveats
- **It isn't Roblox gameplay.** The background is a real photograph and the vehicles are vector silhouettes. Video thumbnails that do this would be rejected under "Artificial visuals / External content". Still images are less strictly policed, but the image still sets a false expectation.
- No avatar or character, so there's no player fantasy or scale.

## Reusable Design Rules
1. **Motion = diagonals.** Put the main subject on a strong diagonal toward the frame edge.
2. **Rim light separates the subject.** Light the subject's edges against a darker background.
3. **Show a relationship** (chase, versus, reward, danger), not just an object.
4. Use real in-game renders (Freecam: Shift+P), not stock photos.

## Context
A creature-survival game. The instruction panel says "Find **water** by following the **blue** trail and markers", with a creature mascot portrait. A cyan paper-plane style marker floats in the world.

## What Works
- **The colour word in the text matches the in-world marker colour.** "Blue" is printed in blue, so text and world are linked visually.
- The instruction is **one sentence**, and the panel sits at the left edge, away from the creature.
- The mascot portrait gives the instruction a "voice" without a dialogue system.

## Reusable Design Rules
1. When text references a world element, **print the keyword in that element's colour**.
2. Prefer world markers and trails to minimap or text directions for early goals.
3. One instruction at a time, under about 12 words.

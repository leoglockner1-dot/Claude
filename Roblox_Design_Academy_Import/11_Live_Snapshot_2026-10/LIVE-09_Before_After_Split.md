## Context
- Venom: the frame is split vertically. On the left, a small avatar with a small symbiote and "+1". On the right, a huge muscular symbiote form and "+7M".
- Dance Battle: a split with a red background and a sad default avatar on the left, and a green background with a confident Michael-Jackson-styled avatar on the right.

## What Works
- **The progression arc in one image**: from weak to strong. It sells the core loop (grow by repeating an action) without words.
- The **colour change across the split** (red to green) gives a fast emotional read: bad to good.

## What Does Not Work
- Overused in the "+1 every second" sub-genre, so it signals the genre more than the specific game.
- The Dance Battle image leans on a recognisable real-world celebrity likeness, which is an IP risk (see Roblox's IP guidelines). *(Observation, not a legal judgement.)*

## Reusable Design Rules
1. For progression-driven games, show **start state vs aspirational state** side by side.
2. Use colour or value change across the split so the improvement reads instantly.
3. Make the "after" side about **your** unique growth mechanic, not just "bigger".

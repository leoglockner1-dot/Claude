## Context
- Backseat Rally: a cockpit view, with hands on the wheel, the dashboard, and an autumn forest road ahead.
- Adopt Your Animal!: white-gloved hands reach toward a puppy, with the text "Ages Offline!".
- Anime Dice: the player's hand throws a die toward a boss.
- RIVALS: a view down the weapon barrel at red targets.

## What Works
- **The viewer becomes the player.** Hands in frame turn a scene into an action you are taking.
- It communicates the **verb** directly: drive, pet, roll, shoot.
- It differentiates from the dominant third-person avatar close-ups in the grid.

## What Does Not Work
- It hides the avatar, which loses the "show off my avatar" social appeal Roblox notes is important for many players.
- "Ages Offline!" is a feature claim in text. It's fine for still images, but the same overlay wouldn't be allowed in a video thumbnail ([FACT](../Roblox_Platform_Facts.md)).

## Reusable Design Rules
1. For verb-centred games (driving, caring, crafting, shooting), try a **first-person thumbnail with hands performing the verb**.
2. Pair it with at least one third-person thumbnail in the set so avatar and social context also appear.

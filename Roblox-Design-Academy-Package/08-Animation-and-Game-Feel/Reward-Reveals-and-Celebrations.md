# Reward Reveals and Celebrations

## Tiers (hypothesis durations)
| Tier | Sequence |
|---|---|
| Common | pop-in + small sound, auto-collect (0.3–0.6 s) |
| Uncommon | + rarity colour flash (0.6–1 s) |
| Rare | anticipation (wobble/glow build) → burst → reveal with name (1.5–2.5 s) |
| Legendary | longer anticipation, screen dim, unique stinger, camera move, celebration (2.5–4 s, skippable) |

## Rules
- Anticipation is the pleasure; the reveal itself is fast.
- Rarity signalled early (colour + sound during anticipation) — consistent ladder across UI, VFX, audio.
- Batch multi-opens into a summary grid.
- Paid random items: odds visible before purchase ([Random Rewards and Fairness](../05-Progression-and-Economy/Random-Rewards-and-Fairness.md)).
- Victory screens should move and point to the next step ([V08/V09](../21-Video-Analyses/V08-V09-Victory-and-Defeat-Screens.md)).

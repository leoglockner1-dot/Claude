# AV-10 — Movement feedback (dash, jump, landing)

| Step | Specification |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Movement ability start; landing from height. |
| **Timing** | Dash: FOV +5–8° over 0.08 s, back over 0.25 s; landing squash 0.06–0.1 s (heavy landings only). |
| **Anticipation** | Short crouch frame before big jumps (if animation allows). |
| **Main action** | Speed lines/trail during the dash; dust puff at takeoff. |
| **Impact** | Landing puff + thud scaled by fall speed. |
| **Follow-through** | Trail fades 0.2–0.3 s. |
| **Sound** | Whoosh for the dash; soft footsteps with surface variation; heavier landing thud. |
| **Visual hierarchy** | Movement VFX must not hide the character's position. |
| **Performance considerations** | Trails are cheap; avoid per-step particles on many players (local player only). |
| **When restraint is preferable** | Constant movement games: keep FOV kicks subtle (motion sickness). |
| **Roblox implementation** | Camera FOV tween on the client; `Trail` attached to the character. |
| **Evidence and previews** | [CS-38 Evade](../../20-Game-Case-Studies/CS-38-Evade.md) (movement-mastery game); [GM-18](../../04-Game-Mechanics/GM-18-Skill-Based-Movement.md). |

**Test:** play it 20 times in a row. Is it still pleasant? Does it still read at phone size? Is the meaning clear with the sound off and with the visuals ignored (sound only)?

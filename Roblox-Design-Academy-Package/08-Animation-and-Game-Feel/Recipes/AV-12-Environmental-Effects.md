# AV-12 — Environmental effects (ambient life)

| Step | Specification |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Area entered; time of day; weather state. |
| **Timing** | Slow loops (3–10 s); fades of 0.5–1 s on state change. |
| **Anticipation** | Weather builds (wind before rain). |
| **Main action** | Fireflies, dust motes, falling leaves, fog drift. |
| **Impact** | Rarely: lightning flash + thunder (sound delayed by distance for realism). |
| **Follow-through** | Settles into an ambient loop. |
| **Sound** | Ambient bed per area, crossfaded (AV-17). |
| **Visual hierarchy** | Ambient effects stay low-contrast so gameplay cues stand out. |
| **Performance considerations** | Biggest risk of overdraw; few large, slow particles beat many small ones; stream them out with distance. |
| **When restraint is preferable** | Combat areas: thin out ambience to keep readability. |
| **Roblox implementation** | Emitters on large invisible parts near the player; Atmosphere and Lighting properties for mood. |
| **Evidence and previews** | [VFX Principles](../../09-VFX/VFX-Principles-and-Performance.md); [V27 fading particles](../../21-Video-Analyses/V27-Fading-Particles-Volcano.md). |

**Test:** play it 20 times in a row. Is it still pleasant? Does it still read at phone size? Is the meaning clear with the sound off and with the visuals ignored (sound only)?

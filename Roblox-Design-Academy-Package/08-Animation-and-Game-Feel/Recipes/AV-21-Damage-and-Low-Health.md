# AV-21 — Damage taken and low health

| Step | Specification |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Player takes damage; health below a threshold. |
| **Timing** | Red flash: peak in 1 frame, ≈50% by ~50 ms, ~0.3 s total (official HUD video measurement); low-health pulse ≈1 Hz. |
| **Anticipation** | Incoming-damage telegraphs (AV-22). |
| **Main action** | Screen-edge flash; health bar drops (with a "ghost" trail). |
| **Impact** | Muffled thud. |
| **Follow-through** | Health ghost catches up; low-health vignette pulses until healed. |
| **Sound** | Muffled low thud; optional heartbeat at low health (with a visual twin). |
| **Visual hierarchy** | Never cover the centre of the screen. |
| **Performance considerations** | One UI flash frame. |
| **When restraint is preferable** | Damage-over-time ticks: smaller flashes, not a full flash per tick. |
| **Roblox implementation** | Client listens to Humanoid.HealthChanged; tweens a vignette ImageLabel. |
| **Evidence and previews** | [V06/V07](../../21-Video-Analyses/V06-V07-HUD-Meter-Tween-and-Damage-Flash.md) measured flash decay; [Timing Reference](../Timing-Reference.md). |

**Test:** play it 20 times in a row. Is it still pleasant? Does it still read at phone size? Is the meaning clear with the sound off and with the visuals ignored (sound only)?

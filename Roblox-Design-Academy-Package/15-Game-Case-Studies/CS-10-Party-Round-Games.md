## What appears to work
- Rules known before joining; rounds measured in seconds; perfect for tourists (LOOP-006).

## Risks, weaknesses or open questions
- Ratings moderate (66–73 %); repetition risk without variety (inference).

## Lessons for our games
- Familiar rules + short rounds + cosmetic progression = low-friction loop.

## Original application
"Freeze Dance": music plays, everyone dances; when it stops, anyone moving is out; rotating music genres and dance-floor hazards.

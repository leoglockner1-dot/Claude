## Loop breakdown (inferred)
| Layer | Inference |
|---|---|
| Minute-to-minute | Perform a simple physical verb (walk, throw, fish) |
| Most repeated action | Every action = +1 stat ("Every step = +1 Speed", "Every bounce gives you +1 Skill") |
| Progression engine | Multipliers, zones, pets, rebirth, auras |
| Social loop | Racing friends, trade booths |
| Monetization | Very wide catalogues: Speed Keyboard Escape 46 passes (trails, auras, multipliers); Stone Skipping hatch-multiplier stacks; Fish It! luck tiers + battlepass + gacha |

## What appears to work
- The verb is physical and readable in a thumbnail ([TI-30](../02-Thumbnails-and-Icons/Analyses/TI-30-Incremental-Genre-Grammar.md)).
- Massive concurrency (Speed Keyboard Escape 1,322,226) and 97 % likes on two of them.

## Risks, weaknesses or open questions
- Monetization density (46 passes) and luck/gacha mechanics require strict odds disclosure (PROG-005).
- Genre sameness; differentiation lives in the verb.

## Lessons for our games
- Choose a verb that is satisfying on its own before adding numbers.

## Original application
"+1 Kite Height": every gust you catch adds height; zones are cloud layers; pets are birds that pull the kite.

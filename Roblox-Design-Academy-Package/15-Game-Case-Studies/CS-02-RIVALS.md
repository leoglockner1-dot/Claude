## What appears to work
- The strongest visual identity in the sample: red faceless mannequins + one weapon + white arenas on icon and all 10 thumbnails ([TI-08](../02-Thumbnails-and-Icons/Analyses/TI-08-RIVALS-Brand-System.md)).
- Duel pads make challenge initiation physical and visible (social invitation in-world).
- "First to 5" gives short, complete sessions (tourist-friendly).

## Risks, weaknesses or open questions
- Weapon bundles in a competitive game raise pay-to-win questions (cannot verify balance).

## Lessons for our games
- One ownable visual rule beats elaborate key art.
- Make social initiation a world object (pads) rather than a menu.

## Original application
A sword-duel arena where players challenge each other by planting a banner; first to 3 touches wins; every marketing image shows one blade colour on a slate arena.

## Confidence
High for identity; Medium for loop.

# CS-15 — TURBOSTORM Car Racing (DevForum: good sessions, no organic traffic)

> Source: <https://devforum.roblox.com/t/looking-for-onboarding-feedback-turbostorm-car-racing-10-min-avg-session-0-organic-traffic/4951901> (2026-10-23). Developer-reported metrics; not independently verified.

## Observed
- Developer: ads produce **~10-minute average sessions**, but "the second I stop paying for ads, the player count drops back down to zero".
- HUD and lobby analysed in [UI-24](../01-UI-UX/Analyses/UI-24-DevForum-TURBOSTORM-Racing-HUD-and-Lobby.md).
- Community diagnoses: name less discoverable than genre-literal names ("Driving Empire"); video should ground the viewer in 5 s; low-graphics world looks poor on minimal settings ("mimic mobile players"); hard to out-drive AI without boost.

![TURBOSTORM lobby](../Assets/UI/devforum/df-4951901-r5-38cc8c73009b4da016ffa.jpg)

## Lessons
- Session length alone does not create discovery; recommendation needs broader signals (inference from thread).
- Test on minimum graphics — many players see that version.
- Lobby hierarchy: one dominant "Race" action (UI-001).

## Confidence
Medium (community opinions, single thread).

## What appears to work
- Roblox cites Brookhaven as the "playing house" childhood experience, and its **Creator Cam** that hides UI for content creators (SOCIAL-006).
- 641,277 concurrent players; low-friction sandbox.

## Risks, weaknesses or open questions
- Progression is shallow by design; retention depends on social content (inference).

## Lessons for our games
- Tools for expression > scripted content for social games.

## Original application
A "Harbor Town RP" with boats, fishing props and a lighthouse — plus a photo/creator mode from day one.

Medium.

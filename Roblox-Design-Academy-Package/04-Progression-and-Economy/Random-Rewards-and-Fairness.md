# Random Rewards, Odds Disclosure and Fairness

## Roblox policy (verified, paid random items doc)
- Applies to random items bought with Robux **or** with currency bought with Robux: capsules (spins, eggs, chests), enhancement items, combination items, probability modifiers (luck boosts, pity systems, rate-up).
- Show **all possible outcomes and numerical odds** before purchase; percentages sum to exactly 100 %; rounding disclaimer allowed.
- Many outcomes → clickable pop-up with an "Info"/"Details" label; **a standalone (i) icon without a descriptive word is not sufficient**.
- If an outcome can only be obtained once, update remaining odds for that user.

## Fairness guidance (academy position)
- Marketing numbers must match the odds table (counter-example [TI-15](../02-Thumbnails-and-Icons/Analyses/TI-15-Number-and-Odds-Bait.md)).
- Show pity/guarantee progress if used.
- Never hide odds behind multiple taps or scroll.
- Prefer free/earnable random rewards for excitement; sell cosmetics and convenience directly.

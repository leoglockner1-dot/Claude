# Icon Principles

## Facts
Square, ≥512×512; shown as small as ~150×150 (Roblox docs); one icon per locale; default icon is a documented bad example.

## What survived 64 px in the live grid (85 icons)
One face or bold shape filling 60–100 % of the frame, high value contrast, at most a short wordmark ([TI-17](Analyses/TI-17-Small-Size-Champions.md)). Failures: dark, busy, text-heavy icons ([TI-19](Analyses/TI-19-Weak-At-Small-Size.md)).

## Rules
- Subject + genre for new games; logo marks only once a brand is known ([TI-10](Analyses/TI-10-MM2-Logo-Icon.md)).
- No unexplained stickers ([TI-06](Analyses/TI-06-Icon-Failures.md)); event frames only with shared meaning (TI-14).
- Countdown overlays: brief, subject kept visible ([TI-11](Analyses/TI-11-Countdown-Icon-Convention.md)) — 26 % of batch-1 icons and 2 of 39 batch-2 icons used them on 2026-10-24.
- Colour grade sets genre (TI-07).

# Live Chart Study — 85 games, 2026-10-24

## Method
- **Batch 1 (46 games):** top 22 *Top Playing Now*, 12 *Up-and-Coming*, 8 *Top Trending*, 4 *Top Revisited* (explore API, device=all, country=all). Icons + **all** detail-page thumbnails (222).
- **Batch 2 (39 games):** genre-diverse selection from further chart sorts and genre searches (tycoon, obby, tower defense, horror, story, racing, party, co-op, puzzle, fighting, sports, roleplay, survival, simulator), including small games (45–2,000 players). Icons + first 5 thumbnails.
- Viewing: icons at 150 px and 64 px in dark grids; lead thumbnails at 320×180; full sets for RIVALS and Dress To Impress.
- **Bias:** Halloween week (seasonal tags, The Hollow event). Stats are a snapshot, not causation.

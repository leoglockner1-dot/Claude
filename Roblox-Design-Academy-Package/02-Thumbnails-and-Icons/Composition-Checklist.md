# Composition Checklist (thumbnails & icons)

Run on every marketing image. Mark each ✅ / ❌ / ⬜ not checked.

1. **Blur/squint test:** blur 10 px — is the brightest, most saturated, highest-contrast area the subject?
2. **Focal count:** one main subject (+ at most one secondary).
3. **Silhouette:** fill the subject black — still recognisable?
4. **Verb:** can a stranger say "a game where you ___" in 1 s?
5. **Stakes/aspiration** visible?
6. **Eye path:** diagonals, gaze and arrows lead to the subject.
7. **Value structure:** reads in greyscale.
8. **Edges & bottom band:** nothing essential touches edges or the bottom ~15 %.
9. **Text:** ≤3–4 words, large, high contrast, or none; no claims/ads.
10. **Authenticity:** in-engine visuals, no fake UI.
11. **Grid test:** at 320×180 (thumbnail) and 150 / 64 px (icon) between genre competitors — distinguishable?
12. **Identity:** consistent with the game's visual rule and other thumbnails.

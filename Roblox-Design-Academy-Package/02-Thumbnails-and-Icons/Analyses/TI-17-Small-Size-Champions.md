| MM2 | Abstract logo on pure black |
| Illegal Soccer | Avatar face plus a football, clear silhouettes, sky-blue background |
| Backseat Rally | One car, side-on, on a bright background |
| Brookhaven | Wordmark sign plus a door frame; strong red/white text plate |

## Shared traits
1. **One subject** that fills about 60–100% of the frame.
2. **A face with big eyes** (character, pet or creature), or **one bold shape**.
3. **High value contrast** between subject and background.
4. **At most one** text element: a short wordmark, never a sentence.

## Original application
Test icons at 64 px in a grid; one face/shape, high contrast.

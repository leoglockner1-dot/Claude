## What is observable
Thumbnail: the giant guard "Barry" holding a baton in front of a prison cell ("MOST EVIL PRISONERS CELL"), with a **fake menu selector "PRISON < PLAY >"** at the bottom. Icon: extreme close-up of the guard's face with police cap and whistle.

## What works
- The character is a strong, ownable face (memorable villain).
- Icon crop to the face reads at 64 px.

## Why it might work
Recognisable antagonist faces work as brand marks in obby/escape genres.

## What does not work / trade-offs
- The "PLAY" selector imitates a clickable UI inside a static image — it may mislead and it sits at the **bottom**, where Roblox warns metadata can cover content.

## Reusable principles
- **THUMB-010** Do not paint fake interface elements into marketing images; keep the bottom clear.

Show the escape moment instead: the player squeezing through the cell bars while Barry turns around.

## Context
One composition (a lone tree and a small white chapel on a hill) in three grades:
- **A**: saturated blue sky, which reads as dreamy or fantasy.
- **B**: desaturated grey-blue, which reads as somber or moody.
- **C**: red monochrome with a glowing red chapel, which reads as horror.

## What Works
- It isolates **colour as a variable**. Composition and subject stay the same, and genre perception changes completely.

## Reusable Design Rules
1. Decide the **emotional target first**, then grade: saturation is energy, hue is mood, contrast is intensity.
2. Horror: a narrow hue range plus one glowing element. Calm: high-key and saturated blues and greens. Somber: low saturation.
3. Thumbnail and icon grading must match the in-game lighting mood (honesty plus consistency).

## Original application
Grade the same key art three ways and test which mood matches the game.

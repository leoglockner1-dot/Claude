## Context
The icon is a proper orange "TYPER TOWN" wordmark with Halloween decoration. The lead thumbnail served by the API is the **grey Roblox placeholder** (dark grey with a faint Roblox logo).

## Why it matters
A game that reached a chart still shows no thumbnail on its detail page. Every visitor who clicks through sees nothing about the game.

## Rule
Before any promotion or update push, **verify the live detail page** in a logged-out browser and check that every thumbnail has rendered and passed moderation.

## Original application
Check the logged-out detail page after every upload.

## Context
Huge numbers dominate many incremental and gacha thumbnails:
- DIVINE HATCHERS: "0.00001%" in rainbow letters with a red arrow at a mechanical creature. **Its icon and name say "0.0001%"**, so the claims are inconsistent.
- Anime Dice: "1 in 9,342,584" over a first-person dice roll.
- Steal a Brainrot: "$997,151,167/s".
- Venom Evolution: "+1" vs "+7M".

## What Works
- In incremental and gacha genres, **the number *is* the fantasy**: escalation and rarity. It communicates the genre instantly to players who know it.
- Rainbow or gradient text signals "rare" by genre convention.

## What Does Not Work
- **Inconsistent claims** (0.0001% vs 0.00001%) look careless and undermine trust.
- Rarity bait combined with paid random items invites scrutiny. Odds shown in marketing should match the in-game odds disclosure ([FACT](../../11-Roblox-Technical-Reference/Roblox-Platform-Facts.md)).
- The text takes space from the subject, and at icon size the digits become noise.

## Reusable Design Rules
1. If you show a number, show **one**, make it **true** and **consistent** across name, icon and thumbnails.
2. Use numbers only where escalation is the core fantasy. Elsewhere, show the situation.
3. Never put essential digits at the bottom edge.

## Original application
If a number is shown, it must be true and identical across name, icon and thumbnails.

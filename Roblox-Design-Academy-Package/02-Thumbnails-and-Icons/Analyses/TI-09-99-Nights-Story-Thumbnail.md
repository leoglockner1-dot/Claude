## Context
- Thumbnail: a dark night. A campfire burns bottom-centre. A Roblox avatar sleeps against a log ("zzz"). Behind it, a tall antlered deer-like creature with glowing eyes looms.
- Icon: the creature's face with red eyes, filling the frame on a red background.

## What Works
- **A complete micro-story**: safety (fire) vs threat (monster) vs vulnerability (sleeping player). The game's premise, surviving nights, is understood without text.
- **The light source is the focal point.** The warm firelight carves the avatar and the monster out of a near-black scene. A single key light controls the eye.
- **Dramatic irony.** The viewer sees the danger and the character doesn't, which creates tension and a click.
- **The icon is a different but linked crop**: the creature's face, at maximum contrast. It reads at 64 px.

## What Does Not Work
- Dark images lose detail on small or dim phone screens. Here the fire and the eyes survive, which is the lesson: the *essential* elements must be the brightest.

## Reusable Design Rules
1. Thumbnail = **situation**, not object: protagonist + threat or goal + the tension between them.
2. Use **one motivated light source** as the focal point in dark genres.
3. Icon = the **single most recognisable element** of the thumbnail, cropped tight.

## Original application
A camping-survival thumbnail: a tent glowing from inside, footprints leading up to it in the snow.

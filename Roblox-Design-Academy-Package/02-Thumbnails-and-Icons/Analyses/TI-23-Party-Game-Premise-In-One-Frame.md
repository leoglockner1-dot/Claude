## What is observable
- **Hot Potato:** first-person hands hold a potato with a red **"00:01"** timer amid a crowd; explosion in the background.
- **Musical Chairs:** children-avatars on chairs in a circle; a red arrow and **"ME"** point at the one still standing.
- **Don't Press The Button 7:** a single glowing green button on a pedestal; "Don't Press!" text; sequel number "7".
- **Squid Game X:** masked guard with a square symbol, "SEASON 3 – SQUID X GAME".

## What works
- The **stakes are visible**: 1 second left; you are the one without a chair; a button you must not press.
- "ME" + arrow puts the viewer into the scene.
- Childhood-game premises need no explanation (Roblox notes successful games tap common childhood experiences).

## Why it might work
Familiar rules + visible stakes = instant comprehension; tension promises emotion.

## What does not work / trade-offs
- Squid Game X leans on a TV IP's imagery — originality and IP risk (licensing status unknown).
- Ratings: Musical Chairs 66 %, Hot Potato 73 % — simple premises sell clicks; retention depends on round quality.

## Reusable principles
- **THUMB-003** Show the stakes (time, risk, last-one-out) not just the setting; **THUMB-011** avoid IP-adjacent imagery.

## Roblox implementation
Pose the critical moment (timer at 00:01); first-person hands via a camera attached to a dummy rig; add one label at most.

## Original application

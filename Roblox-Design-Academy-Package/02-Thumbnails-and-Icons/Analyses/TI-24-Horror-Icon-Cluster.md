## What is observable
- **IT WANTS IN:** a horned skull creature with long black claws gripping a door edge, black-and-white.
- **Break In (Story):** a white smiling mask on dark blue night (icon and thumbnail identical).
- **THRESHOLD:** a pale wide-grin face in a dark corridor, red horror-script logo.
- **he ate them.:** monochrome pixel figure with large serif text "He **ate** them" (red "ate").
- **Backrooms:** yellow hallway, a black spider-like creature, a red arrow pointing at it, "UPDATE!" and platform icons (PC/mobile/console).

## What works
- One face/creature, high contrast, dark field — reads at 64 px (IT WANTS IN, Break In).
- The **door/threshold** framing (IT WANTS IN) implies the player's position: inside, threatened.
- "he ate them." uses typography as the scare — distinctive in a grid of renders.

## Why it might work
Horror sells threat + vulnerability; faces are processed quickly; dark fields isolate the subject.

## What does not work / trade-offs
- Backrooms uses an arrow + "UPDATE!" + platform icons — clutter that dilutes the creepy minimalism.
- Many horror icons converge on "pale face on black" — distinctiveness requires a unique silhouette (horns, mask shape).

## Reusable principles
- **THUMB-005** One subject with a unique silhouette; **THUMB-008** typography can be the key art if distinctive.

## Roblox implementation
Key light from one side, `Lighting.Ambient` near black, face lit by a `SpotLight`; render at 1920×1080, then crop icon to the face.

## Original application
A deep-sea horror icon: a diver's helmet lamp illuminating one enormous eye in the dark.

High.

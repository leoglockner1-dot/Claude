## Context
Participating games wrap their icon or thumbnail in a **thick red, drippy horror frame** with "The Hollow" logo in a corner. *The Hollow's* own icon and thumbnail line up famous horror mascots from these games.

## What Works
- **A shared frame creates a visible set** in the grid. Players who like one participating game can spot the others.
- The badge **means something** (a cross-game event), unlike the meaningless snowflake sticker in [TI-06](TI-06-Icon-Failures.md).
- Each game's own art stays recognisable inside the frame.

## Reusable Design Rules
1. Overlays and badges are acceptable when they carry **real, shared meaning** and keep the core subject visible.
2. Event participation is a discovery tool: plan art variants that can take a frame without losing the focal point (leave margin).

## Original application
Design key art with a margin so event frames do not cover the subject.

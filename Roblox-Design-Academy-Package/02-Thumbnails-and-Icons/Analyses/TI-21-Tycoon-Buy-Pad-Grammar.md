## What is observable
- **Mansion Tycoon:** avatar points at a glowing **green floor pad labelled "Garage $25,000"** inside a luxury mansion with supercars.
- **Raft Tycoon:** avatar on a log raft at sea next to a green **"Buy Raft $1,000,000"** pad; icon text "BUILD A RAFT" with the avatar hammering logs.
- **Restaurant Tycoon 3:** clean exterior of a built restaurant ("TEMPLATE 2"), logo; no avatar.
- **Museum Tycoon:** split "DAY 1" (log cabin) | "DAY 2000" (glass museum).

## What works
- The **green buy pad + price** is the genre's core interaction — showing it tells tycoon players instantly what they will do.
- Mansion/Raft pair the *mechanic* (pad) with the *aspiration* (mansion, supercars) in one frame.
- Museum Tycoon's Day 1 → Day 2000 split sells long-term growth.

## Why it might work
Genre familiarity: Roblox notes that matching genre patterns reduces explanation (design-for-roblox). Aspiration motivates the build loop.

## What does not work / trade-offs
- Restaurant Tycoon 3 shows an end-state building without the player or the mechanic — attractive but passive.
- Big absurd prices ("$1,000,000") are genre shorthand; they say little about what is unique.

## Reusable principles
- **THUMB-002** Show the verb (here: buy/build) and the aspiration together; **THUMB-007** split images for long-term growth ([Principles](../../14-Design-Principles/Thumbnail-and-Icon-Principles.md)).

## Roblox implementation
Stage the shot in Studio: place a buy pad with a `BillboardGui` price in front of an upgraded build; Freecam (Shift+P) low angle; lead the eye from avatar → pad → built object.

## Original application
An aquarium tycoon thumbnail: avatar stepping on a glowing "Shark Tank $50,000" pad with a giant tank already glowing behind.

## What is observable
- **The Strongest Battlegrounds** (65,081 playing): a raw in-game screenshot — grey crater rubble, small character, no art direction for marketing.
- **Dead Rails** (39,951 playing): a steam train on a dusty prairie, tiny figure, in-game render.
- **Murder Mystery 2** (1,535,981 playing during the snapshot): busy in-game Halloween map scene.

## What works
- Authentic: what you see is what you get.

## Why it might work
These games have strong word-of-mouth, YouTube presence and recommendation momentum (inference — not measured). Roblox says younger audiences are less sensitive to visual fidelity.

## What does not work / trade-offs
- At grid size they communicate little; a new game with the same thumbnails would likely struggle (hypothesis).
- **Lesson:** popularity does not prove a thumbnail is good; established games can afford weak ones.

## Reusable principles
- **THUMB-009** Judge thumbnails by communication, not by the popularity of the game using them.

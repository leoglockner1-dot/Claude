## What is observable
- **Outbreak Mayhem:** chained zombie brute with glowing white eyes, rim light, orange toxic haze, hazard barrels — a strong, single-subject mood image. Thread: game at **64 % likes**; a reviewer "not sure I grasped the point of the game"; the developer later made "objective mode very straight forward and sightreadable".
- **ASMR Farm (merge):** two cute butter blocks "1 + 1" and a green **MERGE!** button with a pointing hand — the mechanic stated literally. Thread: **5–6 min average playtime** on paid ads.
- **Too Many Pancakes:** deliberately crude MS-Paint drawing of a noob fleeing a tornado/meteor/building — comedic, low production.
- **8 Ball Duels:** photoreal first-person pool cue — premium-realistic; thread: **50–60 % approval vs ~75 % for competitors**, reviewers blamed an unclear first-shot control.
- **100-stage obby:** "Can you complete the obby?" diagonal green outlined text over a pale sky with tiny stages — generic question, unreadable content.

## What works
- Outbreak Mayhem and ASMR Farm images are clear in isolation (subject / mechanic).

## What does not work / trade-offs
- **Strong thumbnails did not fix weak first sessions**: in each thread the reported problem was comprehension or control, not marketing.
- Generic question text ("Can you complete…?") adds no information.

## Reusable principles
- **THUMB-001** Marketing quality cannot compensate for loop clarity; fix the first minute first. See [ONB principles](../../17-Design-Principles/Onboarding-Principles.md).

## Original application
Before commissioning key art, run 5 blind playtests of the first 60 s; only then shoot thumbnails of the moment testers enjoyed most.

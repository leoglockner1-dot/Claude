## Context

- the Halloween Update (gothic doll, candles)
- 10B visits (a pastel cake)

- a red-carpet runway
- a mermaid

## What Works
- **The product is the outfit.** Every image is a fashion shot, which is exactly what a dress-up game sells. The player fantasy is "I could look like this".
- **Consistent premium art style** (soft lighting, bokeh, glossy skin). A distinct identity in the grid, far from the typical saturated-cartoon Roblox thumbnail.
- **Each thumbnail is a theme**, so the set shows the breadth of content.

## What Does Not Work
- **The Halloween Update image is in the set twice** (slots 1 and 2, identical).
- **Out-of-season images are still active** in October: summer carnival, Christmas, a 10B milestone. Roblox's guidance is to refresh thumbnails with each update ([FACT](../../14-Technical-Reference/Roblox-Platform-Facts.md)), and stale seasonal art signals neglect.
- Heavy beauty retouching sits at the edge of "artificial visuals" relative to real gameplay. That matters for video thumbnails, though it's not a rule violation for stills.

## Reusable Design Rules
1. **Thumbnail-set hygiene:** no duplicates; retire seasonal art when the season ends; keep 2–5 *active* for personalization.
2. Show the **thing players come for** (the outfit, the pet, the car) as the hero.
3. A premium, non-default rendering style is a strong identity in a grid of cartoon renders.

## Original application
Audit thumbnail sets each update: remove duplicates and off-season art.

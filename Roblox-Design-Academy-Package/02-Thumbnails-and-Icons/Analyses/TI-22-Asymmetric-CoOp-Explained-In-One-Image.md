## What is observable
- **Be My Camera:** a blindfolded avatar labelled **"CAN'T SEE"** (crossed-eye icon) and a second player as a flying drone labelled **"CAMERA"** (camera icon), arrows linking labels to characters.
- **Webbed!:** two cartoon spiders **tethered by a web line**, swinging over block platforms; icon: the two spiders side by side (with Santa hats — out of season in October).
- **Teamwork Puzzles:** minimalist white/cyan scene; one avatar on a staircase of translucent blue blocks, a second avatar above.

## What works
- Each image states the **role split** or **link** between two players — the defining mechanic of co-op games.
- Be My Camera uses two short labels + icons; readable at small size.

## Why it might work
Co-op games must recruit pairs; making the partner role legible promises a social experience (Roblox: social play drives growth).

## What does not work / trade-offs
- Stats context (2026-10-24): Be My Camera 58 % likes, Teamwork Puzzles 49 % likes despite an award mention in its description, Webbed! 87 %. A clear thumbnail does not guarantee a satisfying loop — ratings depend on the game itself.
- Webbed!'s Santa hats signal stale seasonal art.

## Reusable principles
- **THUMB-004** For multiplayer mechanics, show the relationship between players; **THUMB-012** retire seasonal art.

## Roblox implementation
Two-player staging rig in Studio (two dummies with real avatars), labels as world `BillboardGui`s or added in post as simple tags.

## Original application
A two-player cooking game: one avatar blindfolded chopping, the other reading the recipe aloud — labels "CHEF" and "READER".

High for observations; ratings are context, not causation.

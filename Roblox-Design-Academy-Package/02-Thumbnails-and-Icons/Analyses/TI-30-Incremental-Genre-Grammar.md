## What is observable
- **+1 Stone Skipping:** avatar throws a stone; each skip on the water shows "+1".
- **Gym Star Simulator:** over-muscled avatar doing push-ups with weights, green glow, "+100" floaters.
- **Chicken Farm:** giant voxel chicken, coop wall, "$997,874,672/s", shocked crowned avatar.
- **+1 Speed Keyboard Escape:** (1,322,226 playing) countdown icon at capture; keyboard-key world.
- **+1 Slinky Escape:** countdown icon "3 HOURS".

## What works
- "+N" floaters **are** the core loop (every action = number up) — honest genre communication.
- Stone Skipping maps "+1" onto a satisfying physical verb (each skip).

## Why it might work
Incremental games sell the feeling of constant gain; floaters are their universal visual language (DevForum beginner thread also names floating "+N" text as the popular way to show gains).

## What does not work / trade-offs
- Very similar compositions across the genre; distinctiveness comes only from the verb (skipping, push-ups).
- Huge numbers ("$997,874,672/s") lose meaning when everyone uses them.

## Reusable principles
- **THUMB-002** Lead with the unique verb; let "+N" support it, not replace it.

## Roblox implementation
World `BillboardGui` "+1" with `TweenService` up + fade, spawned per action (same as the in-game effect — keeps marketing honest).

## Original application
A "+1 Kite Height" game: kite climbing with "+1" per gust, the string drawing a visible height ruler.

## What is observable
- **Squid Game X:** masked guards and "SQUID GAME" title treatment.
- **FLASHPOINT:** a red speedster with lightning resembling a well-known comic hero.
- **Cameraman Tower Defense:** speaker/camera-headed characters from an internet meme series.
- **+1 Dance Battle Evolution:** an avatar styled after a famous real-world performer.

## What works
- Instant recognition — viewers know the reference.

## Why it might work
Borrowed familiarity lowers explanation cost.

## What does not work / trade-offs
- Legal/moderation risk (Roblox IP guidelines and Rights Manager exist; licensing status of these games is unknown).
- Identity is borrowed; trends fade.
- Teaches imitation rather than original design (academy rule).

## Reusable principles
- **THUMB-011** Do not build key art on others' IP; build your own recognisable character or symbol.

## Roblox implementation
Read [Roblox IP guidelines](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/publishing/ip-guidelines) before using any recognisable character, logo or likeness.

## Original application
Create an original mascot whose silhouette is as distinct as a famous one (e.g., a lantern-headed courier) and repeat it across icon and thumbnails.

## Confidence
Medium (we cannot verify licences).

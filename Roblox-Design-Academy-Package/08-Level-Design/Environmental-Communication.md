## Layout guidance (general practice, Medium confidence)
- **Spawn** faces the first interactable and a distant landmark.
- **Safe vs risk areas** differ in colour temperature and light.
- **Gathering points** (lobbies) put social features (emotes, leaderboards, shop) around a central plaza; queue circles at edges.
- **Sightlines**: goals visible before reachable; verticality reveals the next tier.
- **Environmental rewards**: hidden coins/props reward exploration off the main path.

# CL-05 — Audio Review (checked WITH sound on)

Mark each item ✅ verified (write how), ❌ failed (write what), ⬜ not checked. **Never tick an item you did not check.** Distinguish verified results from assumptions in the report.

- [ ] Audio actually listened to on speakers and headphones (do not tick from silent video)
- [ ] Feedback sounds start on the visual frame (AUDIO-001)
- [ ] Positive vs negative timbres distinguishable (AUDIO-002)
- [ ] Frequent sounds vary (AUDIO-003)
- [ ] Every important sound has a visual twin (AUDIO-004)
- [ ] Music/SFX sliders exist and persist (AUDIO-005)
- [ ] Beds start with visual changes and fade (AUDIO-006)
- [ ] No sound is fatiguing after 10 minutes (subjective note)
- [ ] Mix: important sounds audible over music; ducking where needed

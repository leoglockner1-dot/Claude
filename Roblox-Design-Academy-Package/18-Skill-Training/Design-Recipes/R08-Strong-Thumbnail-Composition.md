# R08 — Strong thumbnail composition

## 1. Goal
A 1920×1080 image that communicates verb, stakes and identity at grid size.

## 2. When to use
Every launch and major update (2–5 variants).

## 3. Required inputs
Completed brief (Thumbnail-Brief-Template), in-game assets, Freecam.

## 4. Recommended structure
One subject on a diagonal or centred; one key light; one accent colour; supporting context; ≤3 words; bottom band clear.

## 5. Step-by-step implementation
1. Fill the brief.
2. Stage in Studio: real assets, real avatars, pose the peak moment.
3. Freecam (Shift+P) capture at 1920×1080+; vary angle (low for power, high for cuteness).
4. Grade: contrast & colour only (keep authentic); add text if needed.
5. Test: 320×180 next to 10 competitor thumbnails; blur test; greyscale test.
6. Produce 2–5 variants (different verbs/stakes) for personalization.

## 6. Animation guidance
(Video thumbnails) one continuous real take, 15–30 s, verb visible in the first seconds (V02).

## 7. Sound guidance
Video thumbnails: catalog music allowed; no voice-over/lyrics.

## 8. Mobile considerations
Most impressions are on phones: test at small size.

# CS-16 — Troll-tower obby onboarding funnel (DevForum data)

> Source: <https://devforum.roblox.com/t/is-it-normal-to-have-a-30-onboarding-rate/4810040> (2026-08-14). Analytics screenshots posted by the developer.

## Observed (developer data)
- Onboarding funnel 6,327 users: Shop Opened 51.89 % (biggest drop), completion **30.03 %**.
- Floor funnel 1,716 users: **3.13 % reach floor 2**, 0.06 % reach floor 3.
- Before the forced tutorial, only 4 % claimed free rewards.

![Onboarding funnel](../Assets/Other/devforum/df-4810040-03.jpg)

## Interpretation
The forced tutorial teaches a secondary system (shop) with a blurred screen; even players who complete it do not climb — the tower itself is not pulling players (inference). Full analysis: [ONB-06](../13-Onboarding-and-Multiplayer/Onboarding/Analyses/ONB-06-DevForum-Forced-Blur-Tutorial-Funnel.md).

## Lessons
- Fix the core verb's first 60 s before shop/reward systems (LOOP-002, ONB-001).

## Confidence
Medium-High (developer data, causal reading is inference).

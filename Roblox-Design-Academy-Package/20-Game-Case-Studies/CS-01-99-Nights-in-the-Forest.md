## Loop breakdown (inferred)
| Layer | Inference |
|---|---|
| Minute-to-minute | Move through a dark forest, gather/build ("Build a camp with friends") |
| Most repeated action | Survive each night against a stalking creature ("Something is watching you") |
| Progression engine | Night counter toward 99; camp improvement |
| Social loop | Friends build and survive together (description) |
| Monetization | **One** pass: Decorator Pass 199 R$ — extremely light for its size |

## What appears to work
- A **two-sentence premise** + a goal number in the title ("99 Nights") = instant comprehension.
- Marketing tells a micro-story (sleeping avatar, campfire, monster behind) — see [TI-09](../02-Thumbnails-and-Icons/Analyses/TI-09-99-Nights-Story-Thumbnail.md).
- 457,487 concurrent players and 90 % likes with almost no monetization pressure (cannot attribute causally).

## Risks, weaknesses or open questions
- The core loop could not be observed; how variety is kept across 99 nights is unknown.
- Dark scenes risk mobile readability.

## Lessons for our games
- A number goal in the name ("99 nights") gives an instant long-term target.
- Light, cosmetic-only monetization is compatible with a top-tier game (PROG-007).

## Original application
A 30-day lighthouse survival: keep the lamp lit each night with friends; the creature is attracted to darkness; title states the goal ("30 Nights at the Lighthouse").

## Confidence
Medium (no gameplay access).

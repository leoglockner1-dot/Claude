## Critical analysis
| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| Strongest part | **Interdependent jobs** create natural cooperation among strangers; the house gives a personal long-term goal; [DATA] created 2008, 93% likes. |
| Weakest part / quit risks | [INF] Dated visuals; jobs can be repetitive; depends on others doing their part. |
| Essential features | Interlocking job roles, wages, home. |
| Possibly unnecessary | Cannot judge. |
| Minimal prototype to test the idea | 3 jobs (take order → cook → deliver) chained; test whether strangers self-organize. |

## Reusable lessons
- A **production chain split across players** makes cooperation emerge without forced teams.
- A personal space to spend earnings on gives social games a meta goal.

## Original application (do not copy the game)
A post office: sorters, stampers and couriers chain together; wages decorate your mailbox house.

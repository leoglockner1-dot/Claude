## What appears to work
- Clear goal and difficulty identity; replayability by randomisation, marketed directly ([TI-26](../02-Thumbnails-and-Icons/Analyses/TI-26-Tower-of-Hell-Feature-Caption.md)).
- Long-lived (2018) with 29.2 B visits.

## Risks, weaknesses or open questions
- 74 % like ratio — the no-checkpoint frustration is part of the product and divisive (inference).

## Lessons for our games
- A single strict rule ("no checkpoints") can be an identity, if communicated upfront.

## Original application
A descending "Well of Echoes" obby: randomized sections going down, falling resets you to the top, a server-wide timer resets the well.

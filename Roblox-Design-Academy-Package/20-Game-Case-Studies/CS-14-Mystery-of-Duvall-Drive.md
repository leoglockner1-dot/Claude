# CS-14 — The Mystery of Duvall Drive (Roblox showcase, narrative puzzle)

> Source: Roblox Creator Docs showcase write-ups (https://create.roblox.com/docs/resources/the-mystery-of-duvall-drive) + videos.

## Observed
- Event-driven cutscenes with typographic voices and fades ([V21/V22](../21-Video-Analyses/V21-V22-Duvall-Cutscene-and-Respawn.md)).
- "Corrupt spaces" that subvert expectations — the pantry that stretches as you approach; audio bed tied to the motion ([V23/V24](../21-Video-Analyses/V23-V24-Duvall-Pantry-With-and-Without-Sound.md)).
- Objective counter "Items Restored 0/4" at bottom centre.
- Marketing image analysed in [TI-04](../02-Thumbnails-and-Icons/Analyses/TI-04-Duvall-Drive-Horror-Thumbnail.md).

![Pantry with sound](../Assets/Animation-VFX/video-sheets/V23-duvall-pantry-with-sound-sheet.jpg)

## Lessons
- Space itself can be the mechanic of fear; pair motion with audio onset.
- Mask teleports with ~1 s black and blur→focus arrivals.

## Original application
A museum-at-night puzzle where paintings stretch their frames as you look away; restoring each painting stops the stretching.

## Confidence
High for observed elements.

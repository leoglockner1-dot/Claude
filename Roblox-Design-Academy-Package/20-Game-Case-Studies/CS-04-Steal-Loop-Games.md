## Loop breakdown (inferred)
| Layer | Inference (from descriptions) |
|---|---|
| Minute-to-minute | Buy/collect creatures or eggs, place them in your base |
| Most repeated action | **Steal** from other players' bases; defend your own |
| Progression engine | Money/second from owned items → better items → rebirth (Brainrot lists "Rebirth") |
| Social loop | PvP theft creates rivalry, revenge, trolling ("Buy slaps and troll gear") |
| Monetization | Few passes: 2x Money, VIP, Admin Commands 7,999 R$ (Brainrot); X2 Money/X2 Growth (Steal An Egg) |

## What appears to work
- The verb "steal" is in the title — the hook is understood without play.
- Theft between players creates stakes every cycle (LOOP-005) and stories.
- Steal An Egg reached 1,343,038 concurrent players in its third month (created 2026-07-25).

## Risks, weaknesses or open questions
- Copycats (Bike For Egg!) reuse the thumbnail formula ([TI-12](../02-Thumbnails-and-Icons/Analyses/TI-12-Template-Clone-Steal-An-Egg.md)) — identity loss.
- Theft can feel unfair for newcomers; protections unknown (not observable).
- "Admin Commands" passes in a PvP game risk griefing (inference).

## Lessons for our games
- A title that names a social verb sells the loop.
- Base-vs-base theft gives each cycle stakes; design protection windows for fairness.

## Original application
A "Swap the Treasure" pirate game: ships carry treasure chests; boarding another ship lets you swap chests, with a short cannon cooldown protecting new players.

## Confidence
Medium.

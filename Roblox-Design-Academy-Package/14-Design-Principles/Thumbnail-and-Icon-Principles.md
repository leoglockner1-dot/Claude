# Thumbnail & Icon Principles (THUMB-001 – THUMB-012)

Principles for marketing images on Roblox. Specs (16:9, 1920×1080, 512×512 icons, 150 px display) are in the [technical reference](../11-Roblox-Technical-Reference/Publishing-Thumbnails-and-Icons.md).

## THUMB-001 — Marketing cannot fix an unclear first minute
**Rule:** Treat thumbnails as a promise; first make the first minute deliver it.

- **Reasoning:** Roblox measures qualified plays (play-through that lasts), not clicks; DevForum cases show strong art with weak sessions.
- **Evidence:** [TI 29 DevForum Indie Thumbnails](../02-Thumbnails-and-Icons/Analyses/TI-29-DevForum-Indie-Thumbnails.md), [Onboarding Evidence From Developers](../09-Onboarding/Onboarding-Evidence-From-Developers.md)
- **Good application:** —
- **Failure mode:** Outbreak Mayhem: strong zombie art, 64 % likes, players "not sure I grasped the point".
- **Conditions & exceptions:** —
- **Roblox implementation:** Thumbnail personalization A/B tests only after the FTUE funnel is healthy.
- **Confidence:** Medium
- **Tags:** #thumbnail

## THUMB-002 — Show the verb
**Rule:** The image must answer "a game where you ___" in one glance by depicting the core action.

- **Reasoning:** Roblox: relevant imagery of what players should expect; viewers decide fast.
- **Evidence:** [TI 21 Tycoon Buy Pad Grammar](../02-Thumbnails-and-Icons/Analyses/TI-21-Tycoon-Buy-Pad-Grammar.md), [TI 30 Incremental Genre Grammar](../02-Thumbnails-and-Icons/Analyses/TI-30-Incremental-Genre-Grammar.md), [TI 01 Urban Rush Action Thumbnail](../02-Thumbnails-and-Icons/Analyses/TI-01-Urban-Rush-Action-Thumbnail.md)
- **Good application:** Stone Skipping: throw + "+1" per skip.
- **Failure mode:** Restaurant building with no player or action.
- **Conditions & exceptions:** Brand-led giants can show less (see THUMB-009).
- **Roblox implementation:** Stage the core action in Studio, Freecam, 1920×1080.
- **Confidence:** High
- **Tags:** #thumbnail

## THUMB-003 — Show the stakes
**Rule:** Depict the moment of tension: time running out, last one standing, threat behind the player.

- **Reasoning:** Stakes create emotion and curiosity.
- **Evidence:** [TI 23 Party Game Premise In One Frame](../02-Thumbnails-and-Icons/Analyses/TI-23-Party-Game-Premise-In-One-Frame.md), [TI 09 99 Nights Story Thumbnail](../02-Thumbnails-and-Icons/Analyses/TI-09-99-Nights-Story-Thumbnail.md)
- **Good application:** Hot Potato at 00:01; 99 Nights monster behind the sleeper.
- **Failure mode:** Calm environment shot for a tense game.
- **Conditions & exceptions:** Cozy games: stakes can be aspiration instead of danger.
- **Roblox implementation:** Pose the peak moment; one key light.
- **Confidence:** High
- **Tags:** #thumbnail

## THUMB-004 — Show relationships for multiplayer
**Rule:** For co-op/PvP, depict the link or role split between players.

- **Reasoning:** The social promise is the product.
- **Evidence:** [TI 22 Asymmetric CoOp Explained In One Image](../02-Thumbnails-and-Icons/Analyses/TI-22-Asymmetric-CoOp-Explained-In-One-Image.md)
- **Good application:** "CAN'T SEE" + "CAMERA" labels.
- **Failure mode:** Single avatar for a 2-player game.
- **Conditions & exceptions:** —
- **Roblox implementation:** Two staged rigs, optional labels.
- **Confidence:** High
- **Tags:** #thumbnail #multiplayer

## THUMB-005 — One subject, unique silhouette
**Rule:** The focal subject must be recognisable as a solid shape and differ from genre neighbours.

- **Reasoning:** Grid browsing at 150 px and smaller rewards silhouettes.
- **Evidence:** [TI 24 Horror Icon Cluster](../02-Thumbnails-and-Icons/Analyses/TI-24-Horror-Icon-Cluster.md), [TI 17 Small Size Champions](../02-Thumbnails-and-Icons/Analyses/TI-17-Small-Size-Champions.md)
- **Good application:** Horned claw creature at the door.
- **Failure mode:** Generic pale face like many horror icons.
- **Conditions & exceptions:** —
- **Roblox implementation:** Silhouette test: fill subject black.
- **Confidence:** High
- **Tags:** #icon

## THUMB-006 — Multi-panel when variety is the feature
**Rule:** If the selling point is variety/randomness, show 2–3 variants side by side.

- **Reasoning:** Repetition with variation proves variety instantly.
- **Evidence:** [TI 26 Tower of Hell Feature Caption](../02-Thumbnails-and-Icons/Analyses/TI-26-Tower-of-Hell-Feature-Caption.md)
- **Good application:** Tower of Hell 3-panel "Randomized towers".
- **Failure mode:** Single panel claiming "1000+ stages".
- **Conditions & exceptions:** —
- **Roblox implementation:** Same camera across variants; composite.
- **Confidence:** Medium
- **Tags:** #thumbnail

## THUMB-007 — Before/after for progression fantasies
**Rule:** Split images (Day 1 vs Day 2000) sell growth.

- **Reasoning:** Shows the progression engine in one frame.
- **Evidence:** [TI 16 Before After Split](../02-Thumbnails-and-Icons/Analyses/TI-16-Before-After-Split.md), [TI 21 Tycoon Buy Pad Grammar](../02-Thumbnails-and-Icons/Analyses/TI-21-Tycoon-Buy-Pad-Grammar.md)
- **Good application:** Museum Tycoon split.
- **Failure mode:** Overused split with only bigger numbers.
- **Conditions & exceptions:** Make the "after" about your unique mechanic.
- **Roblox implementation:** Two renders at the same angle.
- **Confidence:** High
- **Tags:** #thumbnail

## THUMB-008 — Typography can be the art — if distinctive
**Rule:** Bold, unusual typography can carry horror/comedy tone when it is the most distinctive element.

- **Reasoning:** Text-led art stands out among renders.
- **Evidence:** [TI 24 Horror Icon Cluster](../02-Thumbnails-and-Icons/Analyses/TI-24-Horror-Icon-Cluster.md)
- **Good application:** "He ate them." serif on black.
- **Failure mode:** Generic "Can you complete the obby?" overlay.
- **Conditions & exceptions:** Keep words ≤ 3–4; avoid the bottom band.
- **Roblox implementation:** Render text in an image editor, not as default Roblox fonts.
- **Confidence:** Medium
- **Tags:** #thumbnail #typography

## THUMB-009 — Judge by communication, not by popularity
**Rule:** Popular games can afford weak thumbnails; do not copy them as templates.

- **Reasoning:** Momentum, brand and YouTube presence confound (inference).
- **Evidence:** [TI 25 Raw Gameplay Thumbnails of Momentum Games](../02-Thumbnails-and-Icons/Analyses/TI-25-Raw-Gameplay-Thumbnails-of-Momentum-Games.md), [TI 10 MM2 Logo Icon](../02-Thumbnails-and-Icons/Analyses/TI-10-MM2-Logo-Icon.md)
- **Good application:** —
- **Failure mode:** Copying a giant's plain screenshot style for a new game.
- **Conditions & exceptions:** —
- **Roblox implementation:** —
- **Confidence:** Medium
- **Tags:** #thumbnail

## THUMB-010 — Authentic and uncluttered
**Rule:** Depict real gameplay and visuals; no fake UI, no ads/claims, nothing essential in the bottom band.

- **Reasoning:** Roblox video rules and thumbnail guidance; bottom may be covered.
- **Evidence:** [V02 V03 Thumbnail Videos Accurate vs Misleading](../16-Video-Analyses/V02-V03-Thumbnail-Videos-Accurate-vs-Misleading.md), [TI 28 Barrys Prison Fake UI in Thumbnail](../02-Thumbnails-and-Icons/Analyses/TI-28-Barrys-Prison-Fake-UI-in-Thumbnail.md), [TI 01 Urban Rush Action Thumbnail](../02-Thumbnails-and-Icons/Analyses/TI-01-Urban-Rush-Action-Thumbnail.md)
- **Good application:** Accurate obby video, one take.
- **Failure mode:** Fake "PLAY" selector at bottom; cinematic misrepresentation.
- **Conditions & exceptions:** —
- **Roblox implementation:** Freecam captures, brightness/contrast only.
- **Confidence:** High
- **Tags:** #thumbnail #policy

## THUMB-011 — Own your identity — no borrowed IP
**Rule:** Do not base key art on recognisable characters, likenesses or franchises you do not own.

- **Reasoning:** Legal/moderation risk; borrowed identity fades.
- **Evidence:** [TI 27 IP Adjacent Imagery Risk](../02-Thumbnails-and-Icons/Analyses/TI-27-IP-Adjacent-Imagery-Risk.md), [TI 12 Template Clone Steal An Egg](../02-Thumbnails-and-Icons/Analyses/TI-12-Template-Clone-Steal-An-Egg.md)
- **Good application:** RIVALS red mannequin: fully original and recognisable.
- **Failure mode:** Speedster/meme/TV-show lookalikes.
- **Conditions & exceptions:** Licensed collaborations are fine with rights.
- **Roblox implementation:** Read Roblox IP guidelines before using any reference.
- **Confidence:** High
- **Tags:** #thumbnail #originality

## THUMB-012 — Maintain the set
**Rule:** Keep 2–5 active thumbnails, refresh with major updates, remove duplicates and out-of-season art, and verify the live page.

- **Reasoning:** Personalization tests active thumbnails; stale or broken sets waste impressions.
- **Evidence:** [TI 13 Dress To Impress Thumbnail Set](../02-Thumbnails-and-Icons/Analyses/TI-13-Dress-To-Impress-Thumbnail-Set.md), [TI 18 Placeholder Thumbnail](../02-Thumbnails-and-Icons/Analyses/TI-18-Placeholder-Thumbnail.md), [TI 11 Countdown Icon Convention](../02-Thumbnails-and-Icons/Analyses/TI-11-Countdown-Icon-Convention.md)
- **Good application:** —
- **Failure mode:** Duplicate Halloween image; Christmas hats in October; grey placeholder.
- **Conditions & exceptions:** Countdown icons: hours, not days; keep subject visible.
- **Roblox implementation:** Creator Dashboard thumbnails page; logged-out check.
- **Confidence:** High
- **Tags:** #thumbnail #live-ops

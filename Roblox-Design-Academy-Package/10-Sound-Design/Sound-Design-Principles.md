# Sound Design Principles

> All audio evidence here is **measured waveform data** (levels, onsets, spectral centroid). Nothing was listened to. Perceptual statements are labelled.

AUDIO-001…008 → [Audio Principles](../17-Design-Principles/Audio-Principles.md)

## Summary
- Sync with visuals (same frame) — V16 positive, V14/V15 lag examples.
- Valence by timbre (bright victory 1,655 Hz centroid vs dark defeat 1,150 Hz with 170–180 Hz energy).
- Variation for frequent sounds.
- Visual counterpart for every important sound (V04 A/B).
- Separate volume groups (V05); Roblox now recommends audio objects over SoundGroup.
- Beds for mood, one-shots for events (V23/V24 A/B).
- Restraint: not every action needs a sound; silence before a scare is a tool.

## When silence is preferable (synthesis)
Before a horror reveal (contrast), during reading-heavy UI, for frequent low-value actions (hover spam), and when many players act at once (mask risk).

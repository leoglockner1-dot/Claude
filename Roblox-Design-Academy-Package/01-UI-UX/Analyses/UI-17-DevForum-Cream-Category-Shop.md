## What is observable
Rounded cream/yellow panel. Top: five **green** pill tabs (Platforms, Trails, Effects, Titles, Robux) and a red circular X. Left column: category list with icons (All, Popular, New, Owned, Limited) and a purple "VIP BENEFITS – Learn more" card. Bottom bar: coin balance with "+", a level XP bar ("Lv. 24"), a second coin-like counter with "+". Watermark text repeated over the whole image (author's).

## Community critique (quoted/paraphrased from the thread)
- "I'd suggest sticking with a color theme, the shop category buttons seem a bit out of place being green and … the 'X' button could have been a lighter color." (bleeods)

## What works
- Warm, cohesive base palette; rounded shapes consistent.
- Currency and level permanently visible inside the shop with "+" shortcuts.
- Categories include "Owned" (reduces confusion) and "Limited".

## Why it might work
Balance next to prices supports decisions; filter categories reduce scanning.

## What does not work
- Saturated green tabs clash with the cream system (reply).
- Two currency icons look almost identical (author: icons for coins/levels/rebirths "not done") → currency confusion risk.
- Main content area empty in the shot — hierarchy of items not evaluated.

## Reusable principles
- **UI-006** Every currency needs a unique silhouette, not just a colour shift.

## Roblox implementation
Currency icons from one icon set with distinct silhouettes (coin, gem, star); tab colours derived from the panel palette via Style Editor tokens.

## Original application
A bakery shop: cream panel, tabs in toasted-brown, coins as round cookies and gems as sugar crystals — distinct shapes.

## Confidence
Medium (shop content not populated in screenshot).

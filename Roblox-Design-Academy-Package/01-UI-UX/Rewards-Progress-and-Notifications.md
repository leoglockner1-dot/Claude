# Rewards, Progress Bars, Quests and Notifications

## Evidence
- **Progress as a line** with next reward preview (Jailbreak season pass, UI-04); XP "63/103" (UI-11).
- **Quest tracker + world marker agree** (Winds of Fortune, GF-01).
- **Floating "+N" gains** are the common way to show income (DevForum beginner thread; incremental thumbnails TI-30).
- **Upgrade without feedback** = baseline to avoid ([V12](../21-Video-Analyses/V12-Upgrade-Button-Baseline.md)).
- **Victory screens** need motion and a next step (V08/V09).

## Notification rules
- One queue; never stack modals (PROG-008). Toasts top-centre/right for 2–3 s; critical prompts modal.
- Notifications must say what to do next ("New pet slot! Open Inventory").
- Reward "travels" to its counter (FEEL-005).

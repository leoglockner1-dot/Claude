# TROLL Outfit Tower: a launch spike, then collapse from low retention and near-zero monetization

| Field | Record |
|---|---|
| **Problem** | TROLL Outfit Tower: a launch spike, then collapse from low retention and near-zero monetization |
| **Source** | [https://devforum.roblox.com/t/my-game-died-what-went-wrong/4917693](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/my-game-died-what-went-wrong/4917693) (2026-10-06) |
| **Context** | First game by a solo developer: an obby tower where each stage rewards an avatar item. About 2.5 months of development. Some planned features were cut to ship sooner. |
| **Observable symptoms (as reported)** | Peaked at 230 CCU and stayed above 100 for a few days, then fell to near 0. The developer called playtime and retention "HORRIBLE". The post-launch update raised them only somewhat. Lifetime revenue was 1.7k Robux despite DAU above the top-10,000 benchmark. Most players came from the Philippines. |
| **Cause (reported / suggested)** | Community diagnosis (one reply, not verified): Home recommendations tested the game with organic traffic and stopped when retention and session time were far below benchmarks. Monetization was low because the fun (trolling items, outfits) was available free within minutes, and players left before forming any attachment or need for a pass. |
| **Correction** | Not tested by us. Implied: launch with the retention features rather than patching them in later; give a reason to come back (collection, outfit goals, social showing-off); place monetization after attachment forms. |
| **How the correction was tested** | Not tested by the academy; outcome unknown unless stated in the thread. |
| **Reusable lesson** | Ship the loop that creates return visits, not just the first-session hook. A launch spike is the algorithm's test, and weak D1 ends it. |

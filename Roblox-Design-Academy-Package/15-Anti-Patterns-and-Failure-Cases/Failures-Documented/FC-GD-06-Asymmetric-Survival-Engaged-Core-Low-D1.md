# No One Leaves the Woods: a highly engaged core group, but 3–4% D1 retention

| Field | Record |
|---|---|
| **Problem** | No One Leaves the Woods: a highly engaged core group, but 3–4% D1 retention |
| **Source** | [https://devforum.roblox.com/t/my-game-has-players-who-play-for-hours-every-day-but-the-overall-stats-are-bad-what-am-i-missing/4842120](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/my-game-has-players-who-play-for-hours-every-day-but-the-overall-stats-are-bad-what-am-i-missing/4842120) (2026-08-31) |
| **Context** | Asymmetric survival: Farmers gather, build defenses and survive; one player is the Beast. |
| **Observable symptoms (as reported)** | Friend groups play for hours and some return daily, but the averages are bad: session 5–14 min, D1 ≈ 3–4%, many players leave before a round really starts. Sponsored-ad players left faster than organic players. |
| **Cause (reported / suggested)** | The developer's own hypothesis: the first 1–3 minutes, onboarding and matchmaking. A reviewer rated *Uniqueness* and *Fun* as needing improvement. Low-confidence diagnosis; the thread gave no details. |
| **Correction** | Not resolved in the thread. |
| **How the correction was tested** | Not tested by the academy; outcome unknown unless stated in the thread. |
| **Reusable lesson** | **Averages hide distributions.** A small group of experts loving the game does not mean new players get to the fun. Measure the new-player funnel separately, and treat paid-ad traffic as a different audience. (A separate developer bug report, *not* an official statement, measured the same effect on their extraction shooter: with Sponsored Ads on, average playtime was 11.1–12 min vs 28.0–29.9 min with ads off, session 9.2–10 vs 19.0–21.3 min, and D1 ~3% vs 5–8%, with no build change. A Roblox staff member replied that it was assigned for review: [DevForum 4838351](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/sponsored-ads-traffic-degrades-the-engagement-metrics-used-for-discovery-causing-paid-acquisition-to-harm-organic-ranking/4838351), 2026-08-29.) |

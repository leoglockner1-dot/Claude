# Rising Empires (RTS/TD): most new players leave almost immediately

| Field | Record |
|---|---|
| **Problem** | Rising Empires (RTS/TD): most new players leave almost immediately |
| **Source** | [https://devforum.roblox.com/t/i-absolutely-have-no-idea-why-our-rtstower-defense-genre-game-is-failing/4877418](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/i-absolutely-have-no-idea-why-our-rtstower-defense-genre-game-is-failing/4877418) (2026-09-17) |
| **Context** | Three developers, about a year of development; RTS/tower-defense hybrid. They had already reworked the tutorial and cleaned up the UI. |
| **Observable symptoms (as reported)** | Stats good at launch, then worsening. Players who stay enjoy it; most new players leave almost immediately. |
| **Cause (reported / suggested)** | Replies: a first impression of "this is just another tower defense game"; a reviewer rated *Uniqueness* and *Gets to the main action quickly* as needing improvement. (Short replies; low-confidence diagnosis.) |
| **Correction** | Not tested by us. Implied: make the distinctive twist visible in the first 30 s and in the thumbnail; get to the first meaningful action faster. |
| **How the correction was tested** | Not tested by the academy; outcome unknown unless stated in the thread. |
| **Reusable lesson** | In a crowded genre, the first minute must show what is *different*, not just what is familiar. |

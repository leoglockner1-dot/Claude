# Game-Pass Economy Study (85 games, 1,041 passes)

**Data:** `apis.roblox.com/game-passes/v1/universes/{id}/game-passes` for all 85 games of the [live study](../02-Thumbnails-and-Icons/Live-Chart-Study-2026-10.md), fetched 2026-10-24. Prices are list prices seen by the research environment (US); regional pricing may differ. **Descriptive data — no causal claims about revenue.**

## Overall
- 1,041 passes listed; **695 for sale with a price**.
- Passes per game: median **6**, max **46** (+1 Speed Keyboard Escape); **8 games sell none**.
- Price: median **249 R$**, 25th percentile **99**, 75th **499**; min 1, max 7,999.
- Most common price points: 99 (51), 199 (47), 299 (44), 399 (38), 499 (29), 149 (25), 799 (20), 599 (19), 79 (16), 249 (15).

## Notable cases
- **99 Nights in the Forest:** 1 pass (Decorator, 199 R$) at 457,487 concurrent players.
- **+1 Speed Keyboard Escape:** 46 passes (many trails/auras) at 1,322,226 players.
- **Steal a Brainrot:** "Admin Commands" 7,999 R$.
- **Raft Tycoon:** passes listed at 1–3 R$ (cause unknown; possibly regional/promotional pricing).

## Interpretation (Medium confidence)
Both minimal and very dense catalogues coexist among top games — catalogue size alone does not explain success. Cosmetic/expression and multiplier passes dominate; pricing clusters on 99–499.

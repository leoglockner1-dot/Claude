# Cooperative and Social Design

## Roblox facts & guidance
Social connection is one of three platform strengths; users play with friends, hop games together; Roblox warns against prohibitive private-server prices; roleplay games let players become each other's content; avatars matter to players.

## Patterns observed
| Pattern | Evidence |
|---|---|
| Friend bonuses (+10 % per friend) | Raft Tycoon ([CS-11](../15-Game-Case-Studies/CS-11-Tycoon-Group.md)) |
| Free private servers advertised | Be My Camera, Webbed! (CS-09) |
| Asymmetric roles | Be My Camera ([TI-22](../02-Thumbnails-and-Icons/Analyses/TI-22-Asymmetric-CoOp-Explained-In-One-Image.md)) |
| Physical challenge objects | RIVALS duel pads (CS-02) |
| Judging others | Dress To Impress voting (CS-03) |
| Theft/rivalry | Steal games (CS-04) |
| Trading | Adopt Me! (CS-06) |
| Creator Cam | Brookhaven (CS-08) |

## Genuine social design vs pressure
| Genuine | Pressure |
|---|---|
| Shared goals, complementary roles, visible recognition | Locked content unless you invite N friends |
| Bonuses for playing together (optional) | Penalties for playing alone |
| Free private servers | Paywalled friend play |

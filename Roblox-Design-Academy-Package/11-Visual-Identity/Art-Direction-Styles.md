# Art-Direction Styles Observed (and what makes each work)

| Style | Example | What makes it work | Risk |
|---|---|---|---|
| Minimalist graphic | RIVALS ([TI-08](../02-Thumbnails-and-Icons/Analyses/TI-08-RIVALS-Brand-System.md)) | One saturated hue on neutral field; one rule everywhere | Can feel sterile for social games |
| Premium glamour | Dress To Impress (TI-13) | Soft lighting, bokeh, fashion photography grammar | Artificial-visuals line for videos |
| Atmospheric horror | Duvall Drive (TI-04), IT WANTS IN (TI-24) | One motivated light, dark field, one accent | Mobile dark-scene readability |
| Playful cartoon | Pet Simulator 99, SNACK Defense (TI-17) | Flat shapes, big eyes, bright primaries | Genre sameness |
| Chaotic meme | Steal a Brainrot, +1 games (TI-30) | Absurd characters + big numbers | Identity borrowed from memes, IP risk |
| Realistic | Fallen Survival, 8 Ball Duels (TI-29) | Material realism | Higher device cost; may misrepresent if pre-rendered |
| Hand-drawn / crude | Too Many Pancakes (TI-29), Color or Die sign (ONB-02) | Humour, authenticity | Reads low-effort if not intentional |
| Diegetic sci-fi | Beyond the Dark (V25) | UI as world signage | Legibility |
| Typographic | "he ate them." (TI-24) | Text as key art | Needs strong type choice |

Chaotic and colourful can be excellent when **intentional and consistent** — minimalism is not the same as quality.

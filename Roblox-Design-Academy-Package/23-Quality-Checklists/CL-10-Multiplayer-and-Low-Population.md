# CL-10 — Multiplayer & Low Population

Mark each item ✅ verified (write how), ❌ failed (write what), ⬜ not checked. **Never tick an item you did not check.** Distinguish verified results from assumptions in the report.

- [ ] Playing with friends is easy and rewarded (SOCIAL-001)
- [ ] Roles/links legible in world and marketing (SOCIAL-002)
- [ ] Solo fallback exists (bots/scaling/async) (SOCIAL-003)
- [ ] Bots are honest (marked) and fair (never take rare rewards from humans)
- [ ] Players can express and show status (SOCIAL-004/005)
- [ ] Creator/hide-UI mode exists (SOCIAL-006)
- [ ] Empty-server test: 1 player for 10 minutes — still fun?
- [ ] Griefing/trolling tools limited or opt-in

# Benchmarks and Practical Exams

18 benchmarks: **15 executed** by the academy with evidence linked, **1 partly executed**, **2 specification only**. Each has a time box, inputs, deliverables, pass/fail criteria, a rubric, failure modes and the academy's own run.

"Executed" means the academy did the task in this environment and the evidence (renders, QC JSON, test logs, checker output) is in the package. It does **not** mean a human reviewer graded it, or that it was verified in Roblox Studio.

## Suggested exam sets
- **Scripter (half day):** BM-02 → BM-08 → BM-09
- **3D artist (one day):** BM-04 → BM-06 → BM-05 → BM-07
- **Designer (half day):** BM-01 → BM-15 → BM-03
- **Capstone:** BM-16 plus BM-18 on your own prototype

## Scoring
Pass = every acceptance criterion met. The rubric (max 3 per dimension) is for tracking growth between attempts; record the attempts in the [Research Tracker](../00-Start-Here/Research-Tracker.md) or your own log.
